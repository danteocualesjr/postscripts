My dear Reader,

I ought to begin by admitting that I am not, strictly speaking, qualified to keep notes of any kind. A bachelor, by definition, is a man with too much room and too little explanation. The rooms accumulate silence. The explanations are postponed.

Tonight I came home to find the hall lamp burning though I am certain I turned it off. I stood in the doorway longer than necessary, as if the apartment might confess on its own. It did not. It never does.

There was a letter in the post, unremarkable in every respect except that it was addressed to me in a hand I almost recognized. Almost is a word bachelors use often. We are specialists in nearly.

I have opened no envelope yet. I have made tea. I have sat down to write this first note instead, which is either discipline or evasion, and I suspect the two are cousins.

If you are reading this, then I have done the one thing I promised myself I would not do: I have begun.

Yours, in anticipation,

*The Bachelor*
